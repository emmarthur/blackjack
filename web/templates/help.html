How to play

Classic 21 against the dealer — no splits or insurance in this version

Card values

Number cards count as face value. Kings, queens, and jacks are worth 10. Aces count as 1 or 11 — whichever keeps your hand closer to 21 without busting.

Your turn

Place a bet, then choose Hit for another card or Stand to hold. On your first two cards you may Double: add to your bet, take exactly one more card, then the dealer plays.

Dealer & payouts

The dealer draws until reaching 17 or higher. If you go over 21, you lose. If the dealer busts, you win. Ties return your bet. You start with $5,000 in play money.
